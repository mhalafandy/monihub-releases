## MoniHub 1.0.0

First release.

MoniHub is a personal finance tracker for desktop and mobile that keeps your data on your own devices — no cloud, no external servers. Your desktop and phone sync directly over your local network.

### What's included

**Accounts & transactions** — track multiple accounts, log income and expenses, organize with categories, subcategories, and tags.

**Budgets** — set spending limits by category, with optional hierarchy enforcement (subcategory spending counts against its parent category's budget).

**Investments** — track buys, sells, and holdings alongside your regular accounts.

**Recurring transactions** — set up templates for bills and income that repeat on a schedule.

**Dashboard** — monthly and daily income/expense trends, spending breakdowns by tag and category, top expenses at a glance.

**Mobile companion app** (Android) — full read/write access to your finances from your phone, kept in sync with desktop over your local Wi-Fi. Install it directly from desktop's Settings via QR code, no app store needed.

**Light and dark themes**, matching system preference or set manually.

**Automatic updates** — desktop checks for new versions on startup; mobile receives over-the-air updates for app changes that don't require reinstalling.

### Installation

Download `MoniHub-Setup-1.0.0.exe` below and run it. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning since this build isn't code-signed — click "More info" → "Run anyway" to proceed.
